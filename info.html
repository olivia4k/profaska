Justyna Profaska

Justyna Profaska, PhD, is an architect at Diener & Diener Architects and a postdoctoral researcher at the gta Institute at ETH Zurich. She received her PhD in Landscape and Environment from Sapienza University of Rome in 2025. Her dissertation, Virtuality of Landscape: Atlas of Artistic Manifestations from Real to Imaginary, examines landscape as a social construct through Gilles Deleuze’s and Henri Bergson’s concepts of virtuality and their reinterpretation in contemporary artistic practices. Her research lies at the intersection of architecture, art, landscape, and cultural theory, exploring the approaches that question disciplinary boundaries within architecture.

In 2026, she was awarded a Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship to pursue research at ETH Zurich (2026–2027). Her project, gta exhibitions (2014–2025): Art Exhibitions as Critical Instruments towards Interdisciplinary Architectural Education, investigates exhibitions as instruments of knowledge production and exchange between art and architecture, building on an interest in interdisciplinarity and the potential of curatorial methodologies in teaching and learning about architecture. During her doctoral studies, she also conducted archival research at the Getty Research Institute in Los Angeles, focusing on Harald Szeemann’s curatorial methodologies.

She holds an MSc (2020) and a BSc (2018) in Architecture from Poznan University of Technology, complemented by academic exchanges at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology and Sapienza University of Rome. Her professional experience includes positions at Studio Fuksas in Rome and Wenzel+Wenzel Freie Architekten in Berlin.

Among others, she received a Sapienza Avvio alla Ricerca grant (2024–2025) for her research on Oskar Hansen’s vision for the 1958 Auschwitz Monument, The Road, and organized the international conference series Triggering the Change: Collective Action in the Landscape (2023).
